How to Order

Ordering your favorite campus meal is simple. Follow these four steps to choose your food, make payment, and collect your order.

Explore Our Menu

Four Simple Steps

Your guide to a smooth and hassle-free ordering experience.

01

Choose Your Items

Browse the cafeteria menu and available deals. Select your favorite dishes and decide what you want to eat.

02

Pay at the Counter

Tell the cashier your selected items. Confirm the total amount and pay at the cafeteria counter.

03

Collect Your Token

After payment, collect your order token or receipt. Keep it safe and remember your token number.

04

Pick Up Your Order

Wait for your token number to be called or displayed. Collect your food from the designated pickup counter.

Important Ordering Guidelines

Follow these tips for a better cafeteria experience.

Peak Hours

Lunch breaks and class changeovers may be busy. Order early when possible and allow extra time for food preparation and pickup.

Keep Your Token Safe

Keep your token or receipt until you receive your food. Your token number helps staff identify your order.

Pickup Rules

Collect your order from the designated counter when your number is called. Avoid crowding the pickup area and check your items before leaving.

Need Assistance?

If your order is delayed or an item is missing, show your token or receipt to cafeteria staff for assistance.
